\subsection*{Conclusion}
\begin{frame}
    \frametitle{Closing Remarks}
    \framesubtitle{Conclusion}
    \small
    \begin{itemize}
        \item Context is important for a prediction. Salient information is found in the background.
        \item Pooling matters. Small changes lead to enhanced interpretations, with little penalty to predictive power.
        \item Noisy gradients are a byproduct of training. Negative values act as regularizers.
        \item Insertion \& Deletion are prolematic. OOD data affects the classifier.
    \end{itemize}
\end{frame}
